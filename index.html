Privacy Policy for Aether GPS

Effective Date: March 23, 2026

1. Overview

Aether GPS ("the Application") is an advanced synthetic location simulator, developer testing tool, and telemetry engine developed and published by Syaiful Wachid (SWD SOFT DEVELOPER) based in Lumajang, Jawa Timur, Indonesia. Your privacy is fundamental to our development philosophy.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

No Data Collected or Shared: Aether GPS does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data, hardware identifiers, phone numbers, or analytics to external servers or third parties. All operational telemetry remains 100% local on your device.

3. Location Permission and Telemetry

The Application requests ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION permissions solely for core functionality:

  • To display local satellite telemetry and physical hardware GPS fixes.
  • To provide simulated mock location fixes for developer testing and QA location verification.
  • To manage synthetic route navigation locally on your device.
  • To drive physical real-time navigation bypassing mock providers on the Real Nav engine.

All saved locations, favorite routes, and user configurations are encrypted and stored strictly locally on your device using standard Android SharedPreferences.

4. Map Tile Providers

The Application utilizes standard map tile providers (such as Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, and ArcGIS Esri World Imagery) to render map tiles. These requests process standard HTTP tile requests to display map graphics and do not track or store your personal identity.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for developers, QA testers, and adult users (aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect information from children.
